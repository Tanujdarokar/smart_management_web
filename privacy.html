Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

SmartTask Manager is a browser-based productivity app. This notice describes how the app currently handles information. It is a project disclosure, not legal advice or a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific privacy review.

Information stored on your device

Tasks, profile details, settings, and other app content are stored in your browser's local storage. The app does not currently send that workspace data to a SmartTask server. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to access its stored data. Do not use this app to store information that requires server-side protection.

Registration and sign-in are implemented entirely in the browser; they are not a secure, server-backed account system. Passwords are Base64-encoded in browser storage, which is reversible and is not encryption. Do not reuse a sensitive password here.

Cookies and analytics

The app does not currently set advertising or analytics cookies and does not have an analytics provider configured. The privacy choice is saved in local storage so the banner can remember your selection. If optional analytics are added later, they should only be enabled after consent.

Third-party services

The app may load third-party resources used by specific features, such as fonts or spreadsheet parsing libraries. Those providers may receive technical request information when your browser downloads their resources. There is no configured analytics or advertising service.

Your choices and deletion

You can remove app data using your browser's site-data controls. Clearing local storage for this site deletes the workspace saved in that browser. You can also clear the local storage entry named smarttask_privacy_consent to show the privacy choice again. Removing site data cannot be undone.

Changes and contact

This policy should be reviewed and updated before a public production launch, including adding the operator's contact details and any applicable jurisdiction-specific disclosures. No contact address or analytics provider has been configured in this project.
